# AMOR CANTUS AVIUM

*Love of the birds' song.*

Here's what it is.

You're walking somewhere one morning and you hear a bird sing. Not anything special — just a regular bird — but for some reason it lands in your chest and your eyes sting and you have no idea why. The world has been making that sound your entire life. You just heard it for the first time.

That moment is the whole thing. Everything else extends from it.

I'm trying to love being alive. Not in a *gratitude journal* way. In a real way. The awe and the irritation. The tender stuff and the boring stuff. Traffic jams and bug bites and quiet evenings. All of it is mine, and I only get one shot at any of it.

The soft parts stay. Tenderness, awe, getting choked up at a bird. Crying at things grown men aren't supposed to cry at. I'm done pretending those don't happen.

Two things can be true at once. Something sucks. Fine. And underneath that — *I'm still glad I got to be here for it.* Both real. Neither cancels the other.

My life is my own. Howard Roark energy — no veto power handed to anyone over my time or what I do with it. People who matter will get it or they won't. The decision stays mine.

I leave small marks that prove I was here paying attention. Notebooks. Photographs. A journal nobody else will read. Witnesses to my own existence.

Solitude doesn't need to be a lie about loneliness. Both are real. The lonely night and the love of the lonely night are not in contradiction.

Reality gets a yes from me. Not because it's perfect, but because *this* is what's actually happening. Messy, beautiful, annoying, fleeting.

This isn't constant happiness. It's not forced positivity. It's a steady, consuming love of being alive that grows strongest in the quiet hours. The difference between letting life happen to you and loving the act of living it.

A man who can lie on the floor when he needs to. Take too long at the gym. Stop to smell a flower like he means it. Move jobs against advice. Hear a bird and almost cry. And still feel grateful he gets to do all of it.

That's it. That's the whole thing.
