## 9. L3Harris red: v1 values stand, with two unslop corrections

v1 confirmed **`#FF000A`** from the live l3harris.com and proposed full red/teal/purple
ramps. Keep the ramps, but change how they're wired:

1. **Contrast.** White text on `#FF000A` is about **4.0:1, which fails 4.5:1.**
   - For a filled button with white text, use **`--red-600 #d60009`** (≈5.4:1).
   - Or keep `#FF000A` and put dark text on it (≈5.3:1 with black).
   - Teal `#00dcc8` already uses dark text (the login button). Fine.
   - Purple `#a855f7` with white text is ≈3.96:1, which **fails**. Use `--purple-700 #6d2eab` for fills.
2. **Red as accent vs red as loss.**
   - On `/investing`, losses are red. On that page, either:
     - pin the accent to teal or neutral regardless of the user's choice, or
     - move losses to a toned-down red that clearly differs from the brand red (for example the unslop semantic `#e5484d`).
   - Never use the `#FF000A` accent for "down" values.

### 11.2 Direction (needs your OK before any edit)

- **Product.** A music visualizer that paints Milkdrop scenes to whatever you're playing on Spotify, plus a few personal tools (SPX options, routines) behind one login.

- **World.** A VJ booth at night: black gear with small backlit labels, where the screen is the only real light.
